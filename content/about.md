## Hi, I'm Kollin.

I'm a senior data engineer with around five years of experience building and operating data platforms. Most of that time has been spent close to the warehouse — modeling business logic, hardening pipelines, and making sure the numbers that show up in dashboards are actually defensible.

## What I work on

- **Warehousing & modeling** — dimensional modeling, dbt, slowly-changing dimensions, the unglamorous work of making a `metrics` layer actually mean something.
- **Pipelines & orchestration** — ingestion, transformation, scheduling, observability. Less interested in any single tool than in pipelines that fail loudly and recover predictably.
- **Cloud data platforms** — wiring up warehouses, lakes, and the services around them on the major clouds.

## How I think about the work

Data systems earn trust the same way bridges do — through boring repetition and visible failure modes. I'd rather ship a smaller pipeline that the team understands than a clever one nobody will touch six months later. Most "data quality" problems I've seen were really ownership problems in disguise.

## What's next

I'm exploring a move into independent analytics and cloud consulting — helping smaller teams stand up the data foundations they need without the overhead of hiring a full platform team. If that's something you're thinking about, [get in touch](mailto:kollin@hey.com).
