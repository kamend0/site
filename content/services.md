I take on a small number of consulting engagements at a time, focused on analytics and cloud data platforms. Typical shapes of work:

### Data platform audits
A short engagement — usually a couple of weeks — to assess an existing warehouse, pipelines, and modeling layer. You get a written report covering what's working, what's brittle, and a prioritized plan for what to fix first.

### Pipeline & warehouse buildouts
Longer engagements to design and ship a working data foundation: ingestion, transformation, modeling, and the operational tooling around them. Sized to your team, not to mine.

### Advisory & fractional data engineering
Ongoing, lower-bandwidth support for founders and small teams who need a senior data voice in the room without a full-time hire.
